Privacy Policy

Telegram Torrent Drive is a personal tool used to transfer user-provided torrent downloads to the user's Google Drive account.

Information We Access

The application may access Google Drive files and folders necessary to upload and manage files requested by the user.

Data Storage

The application does not sell or share user data with third parties. Google Drive files remain in the user's Google Drive account.

Contact

For questions about this application, contact the application owner through the email address associated with the Google Cloud project.
